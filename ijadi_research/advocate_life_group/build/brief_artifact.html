Plain-English glossary

Terms used in this brief

Google Business ProfileYour free listing on Google Maps and in local search results: name, address or service area, hours, phone, photos and reviews.
IULIndexed universal life: permanent life insurance whose cash value is credited based on a market index, within set limits.
FERSThe Federal Employees Retirement System, the pension and savings plan for most US federal workers.
Text consentThe wording a person agrees to before you text them. For automated marketing texts, federal rules call for written consent to hear from your business, and phone carriers require business texting to be registered.
TrackingFree tools such as Google Analytics that show which ad, search or referral each lead came from.
AI assistantsTools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI summaries, which more people now ask for recommendations.
