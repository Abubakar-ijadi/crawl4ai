What we would do first: a speed-to-lead pilot on the leads you already have (2–4 weeks)

  • Week 1: Put the funnel's text consent in your business name and register it for business texting (A2P 10DLC). Open at least 10 bookable slots a week. Add 24-hour and 2-hour reminders.
  • Weeks 1–4: Every new form or funnel lead gets a compliant text and email within 60 seconds. Missed calls get an automatic text-back. Leads who don't book get a 6-touch, 10-day follow-up. You see every lead in one inbox.
  • Success measure: ≥90% of leads contacted within 5 minutes during business hours; contact rate up ≥10 points on your week-1 baseline; show rate ≥70%.
  • Easy exit: It runs in your own GoHighLevel account and phone number. If the numbers haven't moved by week 4, we turn it off and you keep the setup. No long contract. Why first: it pays back in weeks, needs no new ad spend, and closes a compliance gap.

What we will not promise

  • Rankings, "#1", or a set number of leads or policies.
  • That AI assistants will name you. We can make you easy to cite, but we can't guarantee it.
  • Fake reviews, reviews requested only from happy clients, or bought contact lists.

What you'll see from us every month

  • One page: leads by source, median response time, contact / appointment / show rates, policies, search and map views, and the same AI questions re-run with dates.
  • What we did, what's next, and a clear stop/go call. Month to month.
  • We know two earlier vendors didn't deliver. You own every account, login, page and phone number.
Compliance notes (not legal advice): Ohio Adm. Code 3901-6-01 requires life-insurance marketing to say clearly that it is life insurance.
