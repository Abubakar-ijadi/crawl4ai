What we would do first: a speed-to-lead pilot on the leads you already have (2–4 weeks)

  • Week 1: Put the funnel's text consent in your business name and register it for business texting. Open at least 10 bookable slots a week. Add 24-hour and 2-hour reminders. Point "Contact Us" to a working form.
  • Weeks 1–4: Every new lead gets a compliant text and email within 60 seconds. Missed calls get an automatic text-back. Leads who don't book get a 6-touch, 10-day follow-up. You see every lead in one inbox.
  • Success measure: ≥90% of leads contacted within 5 minutes during business hours; contact rate up ≥10 points on your week-1 baseline; show rate ≥70%.
  • Easy exit: It runs in your own GoHighLevel account and phone number. If the numbers haven't moved by week 4, we switch it off and you keep the setup. Why first: it pays back in weeks, needs no new ad spend, and closes a compliance gap.

What we will not promise

  • Rankings, "#1", or a set number of leads, appointments or policies.
  • That AI assistants will name you. We can make you easier to cite; we can't guarantee it.
  • Fake reviews, reviews requested only from happy clients, bought contact lists, or claims your license and carriers don't support.

What you'll see from us every month

  • One page: leads by source, median response time, contact / appointment / show rates, policies (you enter them), search and map views, and the same AI questions re-run with dates.
